Who to follow

This site stands on the work of people who taught Fabric in public first. If you are learning, follow them too. Checked October 2026.

  • Marco Russo and Alberto Ferrari

    SQLBI

    The reference for DAX and tabular modelling. Their articles, DAX Patterns and dax.guide are where this site checks anything about measures and models.

  • Adam Saxton and Patrick LeBlanc

    Guy in a Cube

    Weekly videos on Power BI and Fabric plus a live Saturday session for questions. Proof that showing up every week builds a community.

  • Reza Rad

    RADACAD

    Microsoft Regional Director, author of more than 16 books, and a regular FabCon speaker. His Fabric Insider interviews show how other practitioners actually work.

  • Will Needham

    Learn Microsoft Fabric

    Runs one of the largest Fabric learning communities and teaches through hands-on weekly projects.

  • Kurt Buhler

    Tabular Editor and Data Goblins

    Leads innovation at Tabular Editor and publishes open-source agent skills for Power BI and Fabric development.

  • Chris Webb

    Crossjoin

    Long-running blog on Power BI, Power Query and performance. Each post proves its point with a measurement.

  • Sandeep Pawar

    Fabric Guru

    Fabric MVP writing about Semantic Link and Python tooling, with notebooks you can run yourself.

  • Kevin Chant

    CI/CD for Fabric

    The go-to voice on Git, deployment pipelines, fabric-cli and fabric-cicd, taught as multi-part series.

  • Amit Chandak

    Fabric Super User

    MVP from India with a steady stream of short tutorials on single Fabric Warehouse features.

  • Erwin de Kreuk

    Governance

    MVP covering governance, including lineage from Fabric lakehouses into Microsoft Purview.

Microsoft features community writers every month in the Fabric Influencers Spotlight. It is the best single place to find new voices.
