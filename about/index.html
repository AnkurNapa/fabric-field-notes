Why this site exists

I started on the brewhouse floor, then worked across distilling and winemaking. Somewhere between a stuck ferment and a spreadsheet nobody trusted, I started building the data side myself, and kept going until I earned the DP-600 Fabric Analytics Engineer certification.

Most Fabric content is written either by people who know the product or by people who know the plant. I try to write from both sides: what the documentation says, what actually happens when you build it, and where it breaks.

I lead the Bangalore Microsoft Fabric User Group, where we learn this together in free live sessions. This site is for that group and for anyone teaching themselves Fabric in the evenings.

How the notes are written

  • Every walkthrough says which month of Fabric it describes, and whether I have run it end to end or checked it against Microsoft Learn.
  • Product facts come from Microsoft Learn. When I disagree with the documentation, I say so and show why.
  • Examples use synthetic brewery, distillery and winery data. Nothing here comes from a client.
  • When I get something wrong, I correct it in public.
